Infra Arcana: licences
======================

The game: Infra Arcana by Martin Törnqvist, a roguelike set in the world
of H. P. Lovecraft, from gitlab.com/martin-tornqvist/ia (commit
2693b3ca, version 23.0.0).

- The code: AGPL-3.0-or-later (LICENSE.txt). The port's changes to it
  (src/) are under the same licence; the source of this build is in
  DalsinAI/amigainfraarcana on GitHub.
- The pictures and tiles (gfx/): upstream's own, under LICENSE.txt; the
  font pictures under LICENSE-FONTS.txt and LICENSE-FONT-DEJAVU.txt.
- The sounds (audio/): each one's author and licence is in
  LICENSE-AUDIO.txt (mostly Creative Commons, from freesound.org).
- Bundled with the code: tinyxml2 (zlib licence, LICENSE-tinyxml2.txt)
  and mINI (MIT, LICENSE-mINI.txt).
- The Amiga port's own files (amiga/, AMIGA_PORT.md, package/, tools/,
  tests/) are MIT, Copyright (c) 2026 AmiCradle Team. The GlowIcons are
  drawn from upstream's icon/icon.ico.
